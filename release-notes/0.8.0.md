# Call Bots 0.8.0

- Adjust outgoing microphone volume live in Aloqa, from 0–200%, for one bot
  or all current bots. Mute stays independent, and each bot keeps its volume
  through microphone restarts and automatic rejoins.
- Volume sliders stay out of the way until needed. Use the speaker icon on a
  bot card or **Volume** in the **All bots** bar. The shared control shows
  **Mixed** when bots have different settings.
- Five refreshed recordings in English and Russian: Neil deGrasse Tyson,
  Shakira, Efim Shifrin, Valdis Pelsh, and Roman Kartsev. The clips have
  balanced loudness and smooth loop boundaries, with clearer speech for
  bots 1 and 5.

All recordings are included in the update. Newly started bots use them
automatically; custom voice files in your fixtures folder retain priority.
